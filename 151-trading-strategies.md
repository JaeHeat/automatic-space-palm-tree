# 151 Trading Strategies

A reference list of the trading strategies catalogued in **_151 Trading Strategies_**
by **Zura Kakushadze** and **Juan Andrés Serur** (Palgrave Macmillan / Springer, 2018).

The book gives detailed descriptions — with 550+ mathematical formulas — of 150+ strategies
across many asset classes and trading styles, plus R source code for out-of-sample
backtesting and ~2,000 references.

- **SSRN:** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3247865
- **arXiv (full text):** https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.04492
- Source of the Instagram post: [@quantscience\_](https://www.instagram.com/quantscience_/)

> This is a descriptive index of the strategy names organized by chapter/asset class.
> See the paper for the full rules, formulas, and backtests.

## 20. Infrastructure

Long-term buy-and-hold investment in infrastructure projects (transport, utilities,
energy, telecom, healthcare, etc.) via direct/indirect vehicles. Used to improve
risk-adjusted returns of diversified portfolios, hedge inflation, and generate stable
cash flows (brownfield projects favored over greenfield).
